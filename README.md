# NEXUS EDU

### The AI Operating System for Modern Education

> **Empowering Educators. Elevating Learners. One Intelligent System.**

NEXUS EDU is an AI-powered educational platform that transforms course materials into intelligent, interactive academic resources.

Instead of treating uploaded documents as static files, NEXUS EDU uses **Retrieval-Augmented Generation (RAG)**, **Large Language Models (LLMs)**, structured AI generation, and persistent conversations to create an intelligent workspace for both students and educators.

Users can upload their academic materials and interact with them through AI-powered chat, lecture generation, question-paper generation, presentation generation, summarization, and previous-paper analysis.

---

## 🚀 Why NEXUS EDU?

Educational resources are often scattered across PDFs, PowerPoint presentations, Word documents, lecture notes, and previous examination papers.

Finding relevant information, preparing lectures, creating question papers, and analyzing academic content can require significant manual effort.

NEXUS EDU brings these workflows together into a single AI-powered platform.
